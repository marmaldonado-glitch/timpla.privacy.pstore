<p class="eff">Privacy Policy &middot; effective August 27, 2026 &middot; updated October 16, 2026</p>
<p>This policy covers Timpla on iPhone (App Store) and on Android (Google Play). Both work exactly the same way.</p>

<div class="big"><p>Timpla collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network connection of any kind. Everything stays on your phone.</p></div>

<h2>The short version</h2>
<p>Timpla has no server. There is no address for your information to be sent to, because the app never connects to the internet. This is not a policy choice that could quietly change in the background &mdash; it is how the app is built.</p>

<h2>What Timpla stores, and where</h2>
<p>The app saves the following to a single file inside its own private storage area on your device:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Recipes you bookmark</li>
  <li>A count of dishes you have marked as cooked</li>
  <li>Your grocery list and which items you have checked off</li>
  <li>The ingredients you have marked as being in your kitchen</li>
  <li>Your metric or US unit preference</li>
</ul>
<p>That is the complete list. This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by me or by anyone else.</p>

<h2>What Timpla does not do</h2>
<ul>
  <li>No personal information is collected &mdash; no name, email, phone number or address</li>
  <li>No accounts and no sign-in</li>
  <li>No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage measurement</li>
  <li>No advertising and no advertising identifiers</li>
  <li>No tracking, and no data shared with any third party</li>
  <li>No third-party SDKs are included in the app at all</li>
  <li>No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data is accessed</li>
</ul>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>Timpla is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects none from children either.</p>

<h2>Backups</h2>
<p>If you back up your phone, Timpla&rsquo;s saved data is included in that backup, the same as any other app. iPhone backups are operated by Apple under <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">Apple&rsquo;s privacy policy</a>; Android backups to your Google account are operated by Google under <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google&rsquo;s privacy policy</a>. I have no access to either.</p>

<h2>Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)</h2>
<p>Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data a company holds about you. I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to disclose, sell, export or delete. Nothing about you is ever sold or shared &mdash; there is nothing to sell.</p>

<h2>Deleting your data</h2>
<p>Delete the app from your phone and everything it saved is removed with it.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>If a future version of Timpla ever adds a feature that touches your data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described plainly here.</p>
